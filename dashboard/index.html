Research note 03 Agent cadence / Feb–Jun 2026

More reviews did not mean better decisions.

FINANCEBOT isolates agent invocation frequency as an experimental variable. In this simulator, open + close review materially outperformed hourly review while making 475 fewer model calls.

Defensible result

190 calls → +26.34% for open + close, compared with 665 calls → +2.60% for hourly review. This is a historical paper simulation—not evidence of a general market edge.

What must travel with +26.34%

  • Current-listing-universe bias rather than point-in-time membership.
  • Paper execution assumptions for fills, costs, liquidity, and timing.
  • January selection / repeated-testing risk before this comparison.
  • Short historical regime from February through mid-June.
  • Non-deterministic model decisions can vary on rerun.
  • No live capital, broker-confirmed fills, or prospective outcome.

Selection logic

January chose what advanced.

Five cadence variants ran in a January selection arena. Open + close, open only, and three-times-daily advanced. That makes January informative—but not clean final evidence.

State recovery

Fill recovery proven; orchestration still coarse.

A SQLite hash-chain journal now restores paper fills exactly once after interruption and suppresses duplicate delivery. Pending decisions, model sessions, and tournament scheduling still lack durable recovery.
